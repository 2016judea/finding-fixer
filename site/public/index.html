    Where this comes from. The Federal Audit Clearinghouse (fac.gov), the public record every group spending $750,000 or more in federal money files each year. Minnesota nonprofit Single Audits : of them carried a finding, from groups; of those groups were flagged in more than one year. The finding text and the filed plan are the auditor's and the group's own words, as filed. The drafted plan is written by a model from that text; it never adds a number, a date or a name that is not in the record, and the group fills the blanks. The agency's six-month clock is 2 CFR 200.521(d), counted from the date the FAC accepted the audit.
